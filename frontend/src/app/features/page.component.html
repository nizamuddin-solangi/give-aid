About Give-AID

A practical bridge between people who care and people already doing the work.

We didn't start with a grand plan. We started with a question: why is it so hard to find and support the people already doing good work in your own city?

Make reliable, local care
easier to join.

We believe that the best welfare work is already happening — in community centres, in family kitchens, on school buses driven by people who care. Give-AID makes that work easier to discover, support, and follow.

We don't replace local NGOs. We make their work visible. We don't aggregate donations blindly — we connect each gift to a specific programme need, and we report back on what happened.

01

Proximity over scale

We'd rather serve 200 families well than 2,000 poorly. Every programme stays local, personal, and accountable.

02

Transparency as default

If we can't explain where a gift went, we haven't done our job. Every donation is traceable, every partner is named.

03

Dignity in every detail

We ask permission before sharing stories. We don't use poverty as a marketing tool. People are participants, not subjects.

04

Work with, not for

Every programme is designed with the community it serves. Families choose what they need. Partners adjust what they offer.

How the partnership works

Partners lend reach, rooms, transport, tools or steady funding.

Each one joins a programme where their help can be used with care. We don't accept partnerships that don't serve the people we work with.

01

Application

Organisations apply or are invited based on programme needs.

02

Assessment

We review 6+ months of active work and community relationships.

03

Agreement

Both sides agree on scope, updates, and accountability standards.

04

Active Partnership

Quarterly reviews ensure the partnership serves the community well.
