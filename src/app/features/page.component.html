What we do

We make local NGO programmes easier to discover, support and follow. Giving is pooled only where partners agree it is useful.

Our mission

Make reliable, local care easier to join—without making it feel distant or complicated.

Our team

A small group of programme listeners, community organisers and product builders based in Karachi.

Careers with us

We hire people who can make a useful thing and explain it plainly. Write to careers@give-aid.org.

Our achievements

Our supporters

Individuals, family businesses and teams who choose to keep showing up.

How the partnership works

Not a logo wall. A working table.

Partners lend reach, rooms, transport, tools or steady funding. Each one joins a programme where their help can be used with care.

01 / Northstar

Made school transport boring—in the best way.

Quarterly route support means families can plan around school, not around a fare.

02 / PakWell

Put a health desk where the queue already was.

Mobile clinics now share space with neighbourhood gatherings instead of asking people to travel twice.

03 / Kindred

Kept the books in the room.

Reading kits and catch-up sessions are placed with teachers who know which child needs them next.
