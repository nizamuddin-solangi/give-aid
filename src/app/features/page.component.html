What we do

We make local NGO programmes easier to discover, support and follow. Giving is pooled only where partners agree it is useful.

Make reliable, local care easier to join—without making it feel distant or complicated.

Transparency and accountability

Trust needs more than a good story.

We aim to make the decisions behind Give-AID visible, understandable and open to questions.

18partner organisations assessed against programme and update criteria
46active pieces of work with a named local team
2working days is our target response time for questions and concerns

01 / Governance

Who makes decisions

Programme choices are made with partner teams and reviewed against need, capacity and safeguarding requirements. Major funding decisions are recorded for annual reporting.

02 / Partner standards

How partners are selected

Before listing a programme, we meet the team, check its current work and agree on what updates can be shared safely and honestly.

03 / Safeguarding

People come before publicity

We seek permission before sharing stories or images, minimise personal data, and provide a route to raise a concern directly with the team.

Raise a safeguarding concern

04 / Giving and reporting

What donations are for

Each listed gift is linked to a current programme need. This demo does not process payments; a live service must publish its payment, receipt and reporting terms before accepting a donation.
